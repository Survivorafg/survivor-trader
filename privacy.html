Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers the Survivor Trader website and the Survivor Poster app ("the app"). The app is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Survivor Trader channels.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects an account (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok), the app receives an access token from that platform. The app uses it only to upload videos the owner created to the owner's own accounts, and to read basic account information needed for that, such as the account ID and upload status.

What we store

Tokens and upload logs are stored only on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. We do not collect data from viewers or other users of these platforms.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers, and it has no sign-up forms.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in each platform's settings. Stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer.

Contact

Questions: send a message to our Facebook page SurvivorTraders.
